The Saga
Rivalry Timeline
1943
Barcelona deliver the largest ever margin in the fixture — 11-1 — a record that stands to this day.
1950s–60s
Alfredo Di Stéfano and Ferenc Puskás define an era of Real Madrid supremacy, winning multiple European Cups.
1986–1991
Real Madrid go on a 22-match unbeaten run in El Clásico — their longest in the modern era.
2005
Ronaldinho's mesmerizing performance at the Bernabéu earns a standing ovation from Real Madrid fans.
2009–2012
Guardiola's Barcelona dominate the rivalry with tiki-taka, including the historic 5-0 in 2010.
2010–2018
The Messi vs Ronaldo era elevates El Clásico to a global phenomenon watched by hundreds of millions.
2017
Messi scores a 92nd-minute winner at the Bernabéu and famously refuses to celebrate — letting his shirt do the talking.
2023–Present
A new chapter begins with Xavi's Barcelona and Ancelotti's Madrid meeting in the post-Messi/Ronaldo era.
